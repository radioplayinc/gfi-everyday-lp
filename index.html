    No fine print

    What other processors bury, we publish.

    The industry runs on confusion — “qualified rates” that aren’t, hardware leases dressed as freebies, jargon as a moat. Here’s our position on all of it, in plain English.

    Who typically qualifies

    • US small businesses taking cards in person, online, or both
    • Steady monthly card volume (the X-Ray bands it in 60 seconds)
    • Retail, restaurant, services, professional / B2B
    • Okay with a fit call before any agreement — no instant-approval theater

    Who we’ll tell to walk

    • Businesses we can’t underwrite — we’d rather say no than fake a yes
    • Anyone expecting guaranteed approval or a locked rate before review
    • Models outside card acceptance (we’re payments, not a bank)
    What is Inovio, plainly?
    The decline-fighting and chargeback toolkit we pair with checkout: decline recycling, chargeback alerts (Ethoca/RDR and Order Insight), 3DS2 liability shift on eligible ecommerce transactions, and P2PE/tokenization to shrink your PCI scope.
    What does MDR mean?
    Merchant discount rate — the total percentage a processor takes from each card sale. Your statement shows a “rate”; your effective MDR (total fees ÷ total volume) is the real number. The X-Ray estimates it from your volume band.
    What is decline recycling?
    When a card soft-declines (insufficient funds, do-not-honor), the system retries the authorization intelligently instead of letting a good sale die on the first attempt.
    What is 3DS2?
    3D Secure 2 adds an authentication step to online payments that shifts fraud liability away from you on eligible transactions — while staying low-friction for legitimate buyers.
    What are Ethoca / RDR and Order Insight?
    Ethoca alerts and Rapid Dispute Resolution share transaction data with card issuers so disputes can be deflected or resolved before they become chargebacks. Order Insight does the same inside Visa’s network.
    Is the POS really free?
    The Smart Flex is free under program guidelines if you qualify — confirmed on the fit call and in underwriting. No lease, no “free*” with a 48-month asterisk. If you don’t qualify, we tell you that instead of switching the offer mid-call.
